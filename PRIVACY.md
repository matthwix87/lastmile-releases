# Last Mile: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Last Mile is a free game made by one independent developer. This page explains what the game does with your information. Short version: the game does not collect, sell or share your personal information, and it has no accounts, ads or analytics.

## What stays on your PC
Your saves, settings, lifetime stats, achievements and any face picture you choose for your co-op character are stored only on your own computer. They are never uploaded by the game, except as described under "Co-op" below.

## Update checks
When the game starts, it asks GitHub (github.com) whether a newer version exists and, if so, downloads it from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address when this happens. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that request: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Co-op (experimental)
Co-op uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Relay and Sessions) to connect players. When you host or join, Unity gives your game an anonymous player ID (no email, name or login) and relays game traffic between players, which means Unity can see your IP address. Your in-game name, character look and face picture (if you chose one) are sent to the other players in your session so they can see your character. Nothing from co-op is stored by the developer. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Discord status
If Discord is running on your PC, the game can show what you're doing (for example the mile you're on, the map, or that you're in co-op) on your Discord profile. This is sent to the Discord app on your own computer, and Discord shows it to people who can see your profile. You can turn it off in Settings > Gameplay. Discord's privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
